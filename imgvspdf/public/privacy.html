Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

🔒 Zero Server Uploads: All image and PDF processing (resizing, compression, conversion, rotation) occurs entirely inside your local Web Browser. Your personal files never leave your device.

1. Local Processing Guarantee

Our Local Image & PDF Tool is designed with privacy as the core priority. When you select or drag and drop images or PDF documents into the application, JavaScript runs directly within your local browser memory. No data, metadata, image content, or PDF documents are ever uploaded, saved, or transmitted to any external server or backend service.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

We do not collect, store, or track any personal identification information or user activity logs. Your files are temporary memory objects handled by your web browser and are immediately discarded when you close or refresh the page.
